The Burton at Bideford is a part of the Sustainable Museum project, thanks to the funding from an Arts Council England fund. The Burton is committed to being a space that considers the importance of nature and sustainability throughout the museum.

Use this website to find out more about how different parts of The Burton at Bideford are part of this story. You can navigate the website using the arrow keys and trackpad on your computer, or using the controls on the screen.
